Apart from main character, there are following characters in the game:

1. Neighbour 1, Tom - in his 30s, working hard and partying hard. Friendly to William, but keeps him at a distance, asks him for favours like walking his dog. Self-centered, mostly talkes and thinks about himself. __3D model optional — can always be away from home and communicate via text only.__
2. Neighbour 2, Mrs. Jones - sweet old lady, warm and positive. William sometimes helps her with various tasks; she reminds him of his mother, but in a good way. She spends most of her days in her apartments, watching TV or looking through the window and thing about old times. She goes for a walk twice a week.
3. Neighbour 3, Anna - recently moved in. William fantasizes about her, but they haven't had any conversation other than a simple greeting in the hallway. She's in her final university year, full of joy. She's kind to everybody, a little bit reserved towards William, since Tom mentioned that "This guy is a freaking lunatic" - she doesn't believe him, but still avoids William.
4. Uncle Michael - William's mother's sibling. After his sister passed away, he connected more with William, noticing that he needs support. Recently he became aware that something is wrong with William and decided to visit him — he will arrive on Friday, the final quest for the good ending. __Doesn't require a 3D model.__
