## Bio / essentials

Name: William Enasni (_Enasni_ -> insane backwards)
Age: 41
Occupation: Customer service, currently unemployed (see below for details)

William is a middle-aged man. He's a loner, not having much of a social life. He's mostly in touch with neighbours within the apartment building where he rents a small flat. He occasionally visits /neighbour-1/ and walks his dog, talks with the old lady /neighbour-2/ who reminds him of his mother, or awkwardly tries to talk to /neighbour-3/, a young, pretty girl who recently moved in.

He's had mental issues since early adulthood, but he had been doing fine until recently. He was very close to his mother, but she died from COVID last year. Then, a few weeks ago, he was laid off from his job — online customer service; the whole department was laid off because of AI.

Because of all this, he had a small breakdown last week, failed to refill the prescription for the pills he's taking, and as a consequence, stopped taking them. He's on the edge, starting to lose his mind. The player can help him get out of the madness, or watch him slowly lose himself in the void of his own mind.
