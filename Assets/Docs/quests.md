Story will progress mainly through quests.
Each quest can be completed or rejected, however quests are independent to avoid branching which would increase complexity too much for a jam scope. 
However quests results will be saved (quest1_completed: true/false etc.) which will be used to modify dialogs / text messages from neighbours and so on. 


Day 1 (Monday):

Quest 1 (REAL):
Summary: Upon waking up, William receives text message that his grocery order is delivered and left by the door.
Player has to pick up groceries and put them into fridge / kitchen.
Purpose: player will get to know who to walk and interact with objects.

Quest 2 (REAL):
Summary: Player receives text message from neighbour-1 like: "Hey William, I just wanted to remind you that on Mondays you are taking my dog to the walk. Thanks!". Text message makes its clear, that William only does that on Mondays.
Purpose: Make player go outside apartment, introduce neighbour. Setup for Quest5.

Quest 3 (Fake):
Summary: Player receives text message from neighbour-2 like: "Billy, sweetie, TV works great after you fixed this morning. You're truly a magician. Mrs Jones".
Purpose: introduce fake quests. introduce Mrs Jones

Day 2 (Tuesday):

Quest 4 (REAL):
Summary: Player has a to-do-list task to apply for a job. There is an obstacle - router is not working and it has to be reconnected.
Purpose: while still being normal quest, it introduces minigames - player has to reconnect cables corretly to the router.

Quest 5 (FAKE):
Summary: Player receives message from neighbour-1 to walk his _pet_ (doesn't say dog). Going to the neighbour reveals that in the apartment there is no dog, but cat (or any other animal).
Purpose:  drive insanity
Additional notes: if animals / goigh to the neighbour door is out of scope, raise this, so it will be rewritten.

Quest 6 (REAL):
Summary: William plant needs to be watered.
Insanity progression: if player insanity > x%, plant respond "Thank you Billy" when player waters it.
Purpose: simple daily routine.

Day 3 (Wednesday):

Quest 7 (FAKE):
Summary: Players receives text message warning about COVID outbreak, suggesting to cover windows, because COVID spreads through light.
Purpose: Mess up with player.

Quest 8 (REAL): 
Summary: Apartment cleanup.
Purpose: During cleanup player has to interact with a photo of his deceased mother, which is setup for Quest 11.

Quest 9 (FAKE):
Summary: Player receives a text from building keeper that William needs go to the basement and turn off the power for a 10 seconds.
Purpose: Mess up with player. Minigame idea: remove fuses in right order or find right cable in mess of tangled toghether wires.

Day 4 (Thursday):

Quest 10 (REAL):
Summary: Courier shows up at the door and offer to leave package for neighbour-3 (pretty girl). If accepted, time passes and player has short dialog with the girl. The girl barely remember William's name since she's new here and doesn't really care. 

Quest 11 (FAKE):
Summary: Player receives to-do notification to prepare a soup for his mother. If player decides to complete it, William will take the soup and give it to neighbour-2, who will be surprised and induce big sanity decrease.

Day 5 (Friday):

Quest 12 (REAL):
Summary: To-do notification to assemble additional chair. 
Purpose: Quiet task to give player some space.

Quest 13 (FAKE):
Summary: Player receives text message from neighbour-3 that they should repeat what the did last week, indicating attraction towards William. If player accepts, William goes to the neighbour and despite her rejection wants to spend time with her; things get out of hands, somebody gets hurt (will be fleshed out later), gameover. bad ending
Purpose: Final push towards insanity.
Additional notes: If possible, this quest may have many outcomes depending on current insanity level, let's say if it's medium, player has a choice to back off once she opens a door. If it's high he get creepy or physical etc and player don't have a way to stop it - William mind is taken over.

Quest 14 (REAL):
Summary: William's uncle calls to ask for directions. Player has to tell bus schedule and tell him how he should arrive to his place - minigame.
Purpose: If player isn't insane till this point, this is an entry point to the 'good' ending.
Additional notes: If possible, it would be great if there is some environment hint (letter etc.) that introduces the uncle and notify about his arrival.
