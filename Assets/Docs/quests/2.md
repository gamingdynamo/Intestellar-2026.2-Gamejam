Quest 2 - Let's take Max for a walk.

Type: Go to destination + interact.
Sanity: Real
Trigger: Finishing quest 1 + 1 minute of a real time (to be verified in play testing)
Quest giver: Text message from neighbour.

Summary: Player receives text message from neighbour-1 / Tom to walk his dog.
Purpose: Make player go outside apartment, introduce neighbour. Setup for Quest5 - introducing Tom, and his pet (Max).
Completion: Player walks dog. No effect.
Failure: Player doesn't take dog for a walk - option 2 in text message. Effect +10 insanity.
Insanity progression: None.
