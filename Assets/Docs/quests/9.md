Quest 9 - POWER OFF

Type: Minigame
Sanity: Hallucination
Trigger: End of day 3 / Wednesday, after quest 8
Quest giver: text message.

Summary: Player receives a text from building keeper that William needs go to the fuse box and turn off the power for a 10 seconds.
Purpose: Mess up with player. Minigame idea: remove fuses in right order or find right cable in mess of tangled toghether wires.

Completion: Insanity +10 (and optional +5 insanity if William reply after minigame).
Failure: Ignore the quest. Insanity +5.

Insanity progression: -

Steps:
1. William receives text message (resouce 1).
1. Player goes to the fuse box in the hallway. Interaction with it launch minigame. 
1. Upon completion minigame, power goes off and then it is restored back. Can be reflected by making hallway dark.
1. William inner-dialogue: "That was an odd request, but I guess it wasn't just in my head". 
1. Player can now reply to the text message with "Done!", if he goes he receives another message (resource 2) and gets +5 insanity.

Resources:
1. Text message:
   Sender: 475 886 162
   Date: Wednesday, 2nd September 18:04 (or sync with game time is there is any)
   Content: "Hey, building supervisor here. We're doing important electricty maintenance work. Can you go to the fuse box in the hallway and turn off power for 10 seconds? That will help us a lot, thanks!"
   Response possible: Yes. after quest is completed: Option 1: "Done, power has been turned off and back on."

2. Text message:
   Sender: 475 886 162
   Date: Wednesday, 2nd September 18:14 (or sync with game time is there is any)
   Content: "WTF? Stop playing with power, you idiot."
   Response possible: No.
   After this message is displayed, phone screen flickers and initial message (resource 1) disapears. This is to indicate that it was imaginary / hallucinated message.
