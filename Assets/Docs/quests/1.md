Quest 1 - Groceries

Type: Fetch + Interact
Sanity: Real
Trigger: Beginning of the game.
Quest giver: Text message from courier.

Summary: Upon waking up, William receives text message that his grocery order is delivered and left by the door.
Player has to pick up groceries and put them into fridge / kitchen.
Purpose: player will get to know who to walk and interact with objects.
Completion: Player reach final step. No effect.
Failure: Player doesn't take groceries. Quest marked as complete. Effect +5 insanity.
Insanity progression: None, this is a first quest.

Steps:
1. Player receives text message initiating quest (resource 1).
1. Player goes to front door, open it. Right in front there is a bag, a crate / box or whatever will pass as a grocery order.
1. Player interact's with the bag. Inner dialogue line is displayed: "Should I take this in, or leave here?"
  1. If player takes the bag and goes back, quest continues.
  1. If player leaves the bag and goes back, quest is falied.
1. Player has to go to the kitchen and interact with the fridge to unpack groceries.

Resources:
1. Text message:
  Sender: 768 584 130 - not entered as contact
  Date: Monday, 31 August 6:12
  Content: "Good morning. I'm leaving the order at the door as requested in the app.Thanks <smile'y face emoji>"
  Response possible: No
