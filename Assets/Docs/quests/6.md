Quest 6 - Let's keep it alive

Type: Optional household chore. Find object + interact
Sanity: Real
Trigger: Evening of a day 2 / after quest 5
Quest giver: William via inner dialogue

Summary: William plant needs to be watered.
Purpose: simple daily routine.
Additional notes: if player insanity >= 25, plant respond "Thank you Billy" when player waters it.

Completion: No effect unless insanity >= 25, then +5 insanity.
Failure: When ignored, no effect.

Insanity progression: There is additional dialogue line when William has 25 insanity or more.

Steps:
1. William inner dialogue: "I think I should water my plant, I haven't done it since Friday." 
1. Player goes to the plant (in any room, bedroom or living room, doesn't matter) and interact with it. Water splash sound is played if there is any.
1. If insanity >= 25, additional dialogue happen (resource 1)

Resources:
1. Dialogue:
    Plant: "Thank you, Billy"
    William: "What? Who said that?"
    Plant: "..."
    William (inner dialogue): "Am I getting crazy or what? I need good night sleep."
