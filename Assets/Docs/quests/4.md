Quest 4 - Let's get a job!

Type: Interact + Minigame
Sanity: Real
Trigger: Beginning of a day 2 (Tuesday)
Quest giver: To-do app on a phone.


Summary: Player has a to-do-list task to apply for a job. There is an obstacle - router is not working and it has to be reconnected.
Purpose: while still being normal quest, it introduces minigames - player has to reconnect cables corretly to the router.

Completion: Fix the router and send the application.
Failure: Ignore the quest until end of a day. Insanity +5.

Insanity progression: When player has insanity >= 10, job ad is resource 3 instead resource 2.

Steps:
1. Player receives to-do notification (resource 1)
1. Player interacts with the PC.
1. Screen displays "Cannot load requested page. Check your internet connection".
1. Player goes to the router and interact.
1. Minigame starts, player has to complete
1. Player interacts again with the PC.
1. Screen shows job ad (resource 2) with an 'Apply' button.
1. Once player click apply, quest is completed.

Resources:
1. To-do notification: 
   Date: Tuesday, 1st September, 08:00
   Content: "Reminder: send job application."
